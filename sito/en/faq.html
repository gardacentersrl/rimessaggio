Questions

Is the vehicle stored indoors or outdoors?

All our spaces are indoors, inside closed sheds. We do not offer outdoor spaces.

Is my vehicle insured?

Yes. While in storage your vehicle is covered by an insurance policy for theft, fire, lightning and explosion, vandalism, weather events and damage caused by moving vehicles inside the storage area.

Compensation is capped at 25,000 € per vehicle (except in cases of wilful misconduct or gross negligence) and cannot exceed the market value of the vehicle at the time of the damage. Not covered: damage caused by gas bottles or flammable materials left in the vehicle, damage occurring on the day of collection or return, and wear (tyres, jockey wheel, plastics). Details are in the contract.

When is the storage open?

The storage is open from 1 April to 1 October: during this period you can collect and return your vehicle. From 1 October to 1 April it is closed: you cannot take your vehicle out or enter the storage area.

We receive visitors by appointment.

How do I collect my vehicle?

Tell us at least one week ahead (by email or in the customer area) which day you want it. We confirm and prepare the vehicle.

On the day you find it parked outside the shed, in an open area that is easy to reach, and you can take it yourself from 8:30. If you have not collected it by 17:00 it stays outside and is no longer our responsibility. If you need it before 8:30 we prepare it the day before.

If you tell us with less than one week's notice we cannot guarantee it will be ready; if we manage anyway, a 50 € surcharge applies. Two exits per season are included.

How do I bring it back?

Write to us the day you want to bring it back and leave it in the outside areas: do not lock the drawbar with padlocks or chains, leave no flammable material inside, park without blocking other vehicles and take photos.

We move it indoors by 17:00 the same day. If it arrives after 16:30 it stays outside until the next day and is not our responsibility in the meantime. Remember the storage is closed after 1 October.

How and when do I pay?

A deposit (usually 50 €) confirms your space. The balance is due by 30 September, before you bring the vehicle back for the winter; after that date 100 € is added.

You can pay by bank transfer, at the office by appointment or, in the customer area, by card or iDEAL. Paying in full is what allows you to collect the vehicle the following year.

How do I book a space?

Write to us with the contact form telling us what vehicle you have and how long it is: we reply with price and availability. If services and prices suit you, you become a customer with an ID document, the vehicle registration document and your signed contract.

Every April we write to you to book your space: the deposit must be paid by the date in the email, otherwise you lose the space.

Which documents do I need?

You need a valid personal document (passport, driving licence or ID card), the vehicle registration document and your signed contract, with the privacy notice acknowledged. Existing customers can upload documents in the customer area.

What is included in the annual fee?

Included are insurance, 1 standard wash and 2 exits per season. The space is always indoors and the property has automated access control.

Is washing included?

Yes, the annual fee includes 1 standard wash of the vehicle, done at the first collection of the season.

How is the storage protected?

The property has automated access control.

Can I leave gas bottles in the vehicle?

No. Flammable or explosive materials such as gas bottles may not be left in the vehicle: if we find any, a penalty of 200 € per bottle applies. We no longer offer gas bottle storage.

Are there extra costs?

In some cases: 100 € if the balance arrives after 30 September, 50 € if you tell us about collection with less than one week's notice, 50 € for each exit beyond the 2 included. Transport, if you ask for it, is agreed separately.

Do you also arrange transport?

We can arrange transport of your vehicle to and from the storage. Tell us where and when you need it and we reply with the terms.

Where are you?

We have two sites: Camporengo, Via Galileo Galilei, Cavaion Veronese (VR), and Casalmenini, Località Casalmenini 4, Rivoli Veronese (VR). We will tell you which site your vehicle is at.

How do I contact you?

Write with the contact form or call +39 347 5539170 or +39 340 2782810. Email: gardacenter.srl@gmail.com.

How do I enter the customer area?

Press «Customer area» and enter the email you are registered with: we send you a 6-digit code. There you see your account and receipts, book your exit, upload documents and pay online. It works only for customers who are already registered.

Who are you?

Garda Center is a family-run business: indoor storage for caravans, boats and jet skis in Cavaion Veronese and Rivoli Veronese, on Lake Garda.
