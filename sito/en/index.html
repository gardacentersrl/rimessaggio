When you can collect your vehicle

From 1 April to 1 October the storage is open: you can collect and bring back your vehicle whenever you like, with one week's notice. The rest of the year it rests indoors.

Indoor spaces only

We have no outdoor spaces: every vehicle stands inside a closed shed, dry and protected from sun, hail and damp. We are one of the largest indoor storage sites for caravans and boats on Lake Garda.

Included in the annual fee

  • InsuranceTheft, fire, lightning and explosion, vandalism, weather events and damage from moving vehicles inside the storage area.
  • 1 standard washWe do it at the first collection of each season.
  • 2 exits per seasonCollect and bring back your vehicle between April and September. From the third exit it is 50 € each.

Access. Automated access control inside the property.

How to become a customer

  1. Write to usTell us what vehicle you have and how long it is, including the drawbar. We reply with price and availability.
  2. Contract and documentsWe need a valid ID, the vehicle registration document and your signed contract.
  3. DepositWith the deposit (usually 50 €) the space is yours.
  4. Bring your vehicle for the winterPay the balance by 30 September, then bring the vehicle back by 1 October.
  5. Collect it in springFrom 1 April, tell us one week ahead: you find it ready outside the shed, from 8:30.
